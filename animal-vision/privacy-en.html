Animal Vision Camera Privacy Policy

Operator: PomeLab · Posted: November 25, 2026 · Effective: the app's release date on Google Play · 한국어

PomeLab operates the Animal Vision Camera app and handles personal information as described below. The app has no sign-up, and PomeLab runs no servers for it.

1. Camera, microphone, photos and videos

The camera view, the photos and videos you take, and photos you choose from your album are processed only on your device. PomeLab does not receive or store your photos or videos. Photos and videos taken with the camera are saved straight to your device's album, and when you tap Share they go only to the app you choose.

The microphone is used only to record sound while you take a video. The sound goes only into that video file and is not sent anywhere. You can still record videos without sound if you don't allow the microphone.

2. Information stored on your device

The app stores the following only on your device: your view mode (compare or full screen), whether the app name is added, the time you removed the app name by watching an ad, and a save count (used to space out full-screen ads). It is not sent to PomeLab and is deleted when you delete the app.

3. Ads

CompanyInformation collectedPurpose
Google LLC (AdMob, USA)Advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, ad view and click historyShowing ads, measuring ad performance, preventing invalid clicks. Interest-based ads if you consent

Ad information is collected directly by Google; PomeLab does not receive it. See how Google uses information in ads for details.

  • In regions where consent is required, such as the EEA and the UK, a consent form appears the first time you open the app. You can change your choice any time in Settings > Ad privacy settings.
  • On iPhone, the app asks whether to allow tracking. If you decline, you won't see interest-based ads.
  • On Android, you can delete or reset your advertising ID in your device's Settings > Privacy > Ads.

4. Sharing with third parties

We do not share personal information with any third party other than the ad company above.

5. Children

This app is not directed at children under 14, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14.
